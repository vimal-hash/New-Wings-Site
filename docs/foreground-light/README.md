# Foreground lighting trial — 28 October 2026

The user authorised trying both suggested close-view refinements: faint grazing light on the corridor's upper wall edge, and subtle shading/reflections on the large grey logo surfaces. This is a trial, not a replacement for the named eye-one checkpoint. The pre-trial source and native screenshots are saved in `D:/CodexOutputs/Foreground_Light_20261028/`.

`logo-material.ts` contains the material-only response. The three corridor baked meshes `corridor-wall-red`, `corridor-wall-connecting` and `corridor-wall-caps` retain their original textures, UVs and geometry. Their owned Basic materials receive a small cool-neutral grazing contribution, falling exponentially below the world-space top edge. Strength is .012; falloff width is 9% of the surface height (minimum .4 world units). Normal-dependent illumination reveals the curve without an added line mesh or glow. The original bake remains underneath.

The three Swoosh meshes retain the approved #828d96 steel base, .95 metalness and .32 roughness. In the close views, self-fill falls from 1 to .55 and the already-existing shared cinema environment supplies reflections at .05 intensity. The first, stronger reflection setting was reduced after visual inspection to keep the silver stream dominant. No new environment, light, texture, geometry, shadow or render pass is created. The borrowed environment remains owned by its existing renderer cache.

Both contributions are enabled only in `logoReveal`. They smoothly fade from full at camera height 45 to zero at 145, before the final eye. Earlier journey stages and `logoDone` use the previous material response. The renderer/camera path, particles, LUT, stars, atmosphere, bloom and grade are untouched. Material callbacks update scalar response only on existing renders; there is no new animation/invalidation loop. Source GLBs, baked assets and cached source materials are unchanged. Existing owned-material disposal remains in place.
